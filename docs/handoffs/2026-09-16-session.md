## Review of the day's new code, and what it found

An independent read of the four gesture modules and the finger capture before
the worn session. Five real defects, all fixed:

1. **The tag gesture could not move the tag.** The mod's
   `HudElementSmartTagging._find_raycast_targets` hook *replaces* the stock
   method and returns the cached reticle, which belongs to the weapon hand;
   `tag_role()` only fed a nil check, so pointing changed nothing. Now, while
   the off hand points, the hook hands the whole raycast back to stock, which
   goes through `force_update_smart_tag_targets` and therefore through the
   role. The override remains for the weapon hand, whose reticle is already
   traced and cached. The tester would have reported a threshold problem.
2. **The tag pose was judged from the grip but consumed as an aim.** Those are
   separate tracking channels, so a hand with a usable grip and no usable aim
   handed tagging to a hand with no ray, falling back to stock screen-centre
   targeting: worse than the weapon. It now requires
   `hand_aim_usable("support")`.
3. **Reach took over the interaction target while merely approaching** (0.75 m,
   no grip involved), which changed what the ordinary interact button did, and
   what a tag picked, without the player reaching for anything. The claim still
   exists while approaching, so the grip grace works; the target only moves
   once the hand has arrived.
4. **Reach measured to the unit's root**, which for a downed team mate is their
   feet and for a door its origin, often a metre from the panel the hand is on,
