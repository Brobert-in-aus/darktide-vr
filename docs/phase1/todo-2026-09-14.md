## VR-native input (feature backlog)

Quest Touch gives 11 bindable digital controls against Darktide's 16-control
gamepad layout, and today several actions share buttons or stay unbound by
default (individual stim, carried item and device selectors, reload apart
from interact, inspect, tactical overlay, and right stick left and right).
Body-relative interactions can take load off the buttons. Constraints for all
of these: they replace button presses with the stock inputs, never add reach,
aim assistance, speed or damage; online play keeps the stock simulation and
timings; each is an option with the button binding kept as the fallback;
right- and left-hand dominance both work; accidental triggering during melee
and sprinting is measured before a default is changed.

## Full-body presentation (feature backlog)

- [ ] **Custom whole-body IK for the third-person body.** (14 September: design
  document and pose trace recorder done, `26350e8`, `a506d9e`; next: rig scan,
  then the pure solver on recorded traces.) User direction (13
  September): layering tracked hands and torso onto Darktide's own 3P
  animations and IK clashes too often to finish that way; the body needs its
  own whole-body solve. Today the default is tracked rigid hands, with an
  experimental upper-body proxy (`darktidevr_body_proxy.lua`,
  `full_body_experimental_enabled`) that copies the gameplay avatar's spine
  and arm baseline each frame (`inherit_authoritative_pose`) and applies arm
  IK over it; the history in the 26 August plan (proxy `j_hips` jumping
  0.21 to 0.25 m while its private animation ran, stock gait against tracked
  arms) is the reason for the change.
  Shape of the replacement: a presentation-only proxy rig with no animation
  state machine of its own and no inherited stock pose, posed entirely by the
  mod each frame from the headset, both controllers, the gameplay root and
  its velocity:
  - pelvis from head height, head yaw and root, with a crouch blend from
    physical head height and a lean limit;
  - spine and neck distributed from pelvis to head within joint limits;
  - two-bone arms to the tracked grips (the native `dtvr_solve_two_bone_ik`
    already used by `apply_body_arm_ik`) with elbow poles from torso and hand
    orientation, and the held weapon on the hand socket;
  - legs procedural: feet planted by floor raycasts, steps triggered by pelvis
    displacement and turn angle, stride and cadence from the root velocity
    (walk, sprint, crouch walk, backwards, strafing), two-bone knees
    (`solve_body_leg`), turning in place past a yaw threshold;
  - states the stock animation must still own, with a blend out and back:
    ledge hang, vault and climb, knocked down, netted, carried, stagger,
    emotes, cutscenes; the gameplay avatar, collision, hit detection and
    networking stay stock, and other players keep seeing the stock
    animation (a networked pose is the separate
    [feasibility study](networked-vr-ik-feasibility.md)).
  First steps that need no headset: a design document with the joint map for
  each archetype rig (human, Ogryn), the pelvis and foot-planting heuristics
  and the state table; the pose solver as a pure module tested on recorded
  headset and controller traces (joint limits, no knee or elbow flips, foot
  sliding under a threshold, continuity through turns and crouches); then a
  default-off option in the hub and the Psykhanium. Accepting the look and
  comfort is worn only; calibration (height, arm span) already exists and
  scales the rig.
