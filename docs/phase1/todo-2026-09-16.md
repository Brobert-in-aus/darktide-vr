## Grab and throw the servo skull's flamethrower (user, 15 September evening)

- [ ] **Throwable flamer skull** (Skitarius with the flamethrower skull
  blitz talented).
  - **Status (16 September):** built behind the option "Grab and throw the
    servo skull (experimental)" (`c50bc04`, `darktidevr_skull_throw.lua`);
    unit tests only; worn check in the 16 September checklist, item 1.
  - **Visible:** bring the companion skull's first-person rest position
    forward, so it hovers down and to the left of the player at the same
    height, in view.
  - **Grab:** the left hand grabs it. Functionally this is the blitz input
    bound to grip while the left hand is near the skull, like the belt blitz
    holster: the grip holds the stock blitz input. Holding grip aims the order
    as usual (target preview on the ground), and releasing grip (the throw)
    issues it as usual. The zone follows the drawn skull; the pulse and
    growth feedback work as for holsters.
  - **Throw animation:** presentation only. Reference for the answer given
    on 15 September:
    - `CompanionServoSkullAbility.start_flamethrower_ability`
      (`scripts/utilities/companion/companion_servo_skull_ability.lua`), on
      the server, stores the aimed position lowered by 1 m as the skull's
      `move_to_position`.
    - `FlyingCompanionMovementExtension.post_update`
      (`scripts/extension_systems/flying_companion_movement/`) flies it
      straight at `behavior.max_speed` (10 m/s) and stops within 0.1 m
      (`distance_threshold_sq.flamethrower` = 0.01). There is no pathing or
      arc.
    - `shoot_flames` then waits to reach the position
      (`wait_to_reach_position = true`) and flames for 15 s.
    - So the flight time can be calculated in advance: about the straight
      distance from the skull to the target point divided by 10 m/s (8 m is
      0.8 s). It is not a fixed duration.
    - The engine minion locomotion may ease the velocity; measure the real
      arrival against the estimate.
    - Online, the flight is server-simulated and the client sees it
      interpolated, so a local animation must hand over to the real unit's
      position rather than assume it.
  - **Throw animation (decided, user 15 September evening):**
    - On release, estimate the flight time T: the distance from the skull
      to the target point divided by 10 m/s.
    - For the first 2/5 of T, the drawn skull flies freely from the hand
      with the hand's release velocity, with no blending.
    - From 2/5 of T, blend from that free flight into the real skull's
      position, fully on the real skull by T.
    - Then follow the real unit. Online that is its interpolated copy, which
      is also what a late or early arrival hands over to.
    - Log T against the real arrival, to tune the estimate if the
      locomotion eases in.
  - **Check:** SoloPlay and the Psykhanium: grab, aim, throw at 3 m and 10 m
    (log predicted against actual arrival), a release without a valid target,
    no talent (no grab zone), and the stock blitz button still working.
