# The gait, 19 September 2026

Not worn. Asked for once the legs stopped following the stock animation
(two-bodies-2026-09-19.md, "The animation, and the separation rule"): *"write
a gait, and add things like a small step where needed for small movements.
Make sure 6dof still works too."* This is the whole-body design's step 6
(whole-body-ik-design-2026-09-14.md), written as a pure module with the leg
solve on the copy's own rig, and tested against the constraints a gait has
to hold rather than the numbers chosen.

## What it does

`darktidevr_body_gait.lua`, pure. Each foot has a planted world position and
yaw, and an ideal place: its rest offset from the root (measured from the
copy's own rig at ready: hip-width to the side, a little forward or back)
turned to the root's heading and put on the ground. A planted foot stays put,
which is what makes the body walk rather than slide. It steps when it is
farther than `STEP_TRIGGER_M` (0.08 m) from its ideal place or the root has
turned more than `TURN_TRIGGER` (40°) from its yaw, and the other foot is not
mid-air: one foot at a time, the farther one first, so they alternate. The
step lands at the ideal place pushed along the root's velocity by `LEAD_S`
(0.25 s), so it lands where the body will be. The swing eases in and out
over `0.35 s − 0.03 s/(m/s)`, clamped 0.18–0.40 s, lifting in an arc whose
height scales with the step length up to 0.08 m at 0.30 m: a shuffle stays
low.

`darktidevr_body_mirror.lua` runs it after the neck follow and before the
arms, in every mode that draws a body (overlay, mirror, reflection,
overlaytrue). Per side the leg is two-bone solved from hip to ankle with the
existing `Mirror.elbow`, the knee bending the way the rest pose bends it,
pushed forward along the heading so a straight rest leg bends the right way.
The foot is put down flat, turned to its planted yaw: its rest rotation about
the root, re-based. The ground is the avatar's root height, the simulated
floor under the player.

### Small movements

The design's trigger was a 0.18 m radius about the midpoint of the feet,
which would let the root wander that far before anything moved. The trigger
here is per foot and 0.08 m: an 8 cm drift of the root, a lean, a shuffle
across the room, is answered with an 8 cm step, low. The test drifts the
root 5 cm and expects no step, then 10 cm over half a second and expects a
step as the trigger is crossed, landing within 5 cm of the ideal place, with
less than 60 % of the full arc.

### Six degrees of freedom

Room-scale movement reaches the copy's root at once: the neck follow puts
the copy's neck under the tracked head every frame, and the collider follows
later through recorded inputs (`darktidevr_roomscale.lua`). The gait reads
the copy's root and nothing else, so stepping across the room and pushing
the stick move the feet through one path from one root. There is no
separate room-scale case in the gait, and that is why it works.

### Ground (added the same day)

*"Add a ground raycast so the feet follow steps and slopes."* The gait asks
a `ground_at(x, y)` callback for the floor's height where a foot is put down:
where it first plants, and where each step lands. A planted foot keeps the
height it was put down at, so a foot on a step stays on the step while the
other is on the floor and the legs take up the difference; the swing carries
the foot from the height it left to the height it lands at, with the arc on
top. With no answer the simulated floor under the player is used. The mirror
supplies the callback as a raycast from a metre above the simulated floor to
0.6 m below it, against the static world, with `filter_player_mover`, the
filter the game grounds its own hand IK with
(`player_character_state_ledge_hanging.lua:322`). It is asked only where a
foot plants or lands, so at most one cast a frame; a failed cast is logged
once and answers nil.

The test walks the gait up a 0.2 slope and expects every planted foot on
it, the first plant on it, and no swing below the lower of its two ends;
then across a 0.3 m step at one metre and expects every planted foot at
the floor's height where it stands, a frame with one foot up on the step
while the other is still below, and both planted on the step at the end; a
callback that answers nil puts the feet on the simulated floor. Mutated:
ignoring the callback, and landing at the take-off height, both fail at
`:178: a planted foot left the slope`.

### Teleports

A root that moves 0.50 m or more in one frame (a respawn, the first frame
after a spawn) did not walk there. The feet re-plant under it that frame,
no step is led by the jump, and the velocity starts from nothing. Found by
the test's first cut, which jumped the root 10 cm in one frame and got a
step led by a 9 m/s "velocity".

## Validation

```
build/dependencies/luajit/src/luajit.exe tests/tooling/test-body-gait.lua \
    mods/darktidevr/scripts/mods/darktidevr/darktidevr_body_gait.lua
build/dependencies/luajit/src/luajit.exe tests/tooling/test-body-mirror.lua \
    mods/darktidevr/scripts/mods/darktidevr/darktidevr_body_mirror.lua
```

`test-body-gait.lua` runs the gait as the mirror runs it, at 90 Hz, and
asserts: standing still never steps and the feet sit at their rest places on
the ground; the 5 cm and 10 cm drifts above; walking at 1.2 m/s for three
seconds has one foot in the air at a time, planted feet that never slide, no
drawn foot ever farther from its ideal than a step can cover, both feet
stepping within one of each other, and a landing that leads the body; the
duration shortens with speed and clamps; a swing lifts at mid-stride and
lands exactly on the ground; a 30° turn does nothing and a 50° turn
re-plants both feet to the new heading; a 3 m jump re-plants without a step
and leaves no velocity; a 0.45 m jump is a capped step; bad input returns
nil. The mirror test's separation scan still passes: the gait's one avatar
read is the root height.

Mutated before being believed:

| mutation | caught by |
|----------|-----------|
| never start a step | `:53: a 10 cm drift did not step as the trigger was crossed` |
| planted feet follow the ideal place (sliding) | `:53` the same, since a sliding foot never crosses the trigger |
| both feet allowed in the air | `:55: the small step never landed` |
| control | passes |

## Limits

- Not worn. The knee hint, the foot flatness and the arc height are the
  numbers from the design and will want a worn look.
- The ground is sampled where a foot lands, not along the swing, so a
  foot swinging across a ledge edge can pass through its lip; and a ledge
  higher than the leg can reach leaves the foot floating at full stretch.
- No gait classes yet (sprint stride, crouch-walk stride, strafe foot yaw);
  the one rule set covers all of them with the speed-dependent duration.
- Stopping after a walk leaves the leading foot ahead by the lead, and it
  steps back if that is over the trigger: feet come together, one more step.
