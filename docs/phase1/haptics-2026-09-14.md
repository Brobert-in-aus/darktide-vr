# Haptics plan (14 September 2026)

User direction (14 September): two modes, **Informative** and **Immersive**.
- Immersive has haptics for things like shooting.
- Informative has them only for things like an empty clip or a finished
  reload.
- Plan which haptics exist in both modes and which only in Informative.

The option is Mod Options, Darktide VR, Experimental features, "Controller
vibration": Off (default), Informative or Immersive.

## What each mode is for

- **Informative:** a vibration means *something you need to know just
  happened*. It replaces glancing at the HUD or listening for a cue. It stays
  quiet during ordinary actions, so every pulse carries meaning.
- **Immersive:** a vibration is *the feel of what your hands are doing*
  (shots, hits, blocks, recoil), plus the notices that matter to your body in
  the moment: your hand is in the right place, you are out of rounds, you are
  hurt.

Assumption, for the evening review: Immersive is not a superset. It leaves
out abstract notices such as low ammo, a recharged ability or a menu hover,
so a pulse in Immersive is always about your hands or your body. If you would
rather Immersive played everything Informative does, that is a one-line
change per kind.

## Pulse vocabulary

The Quest controllers take amplitude and duration only; frequency is left to
the runtime.

| Shape | Amplitude | Duration | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| tick | 0.2-0.3 | 10-15 ms | a hand reached something; menu hover |
| notice | 0.4-0.55 | 25-45 ms | something changed that you should act on |
| alert | 0.8-1.0 | 90-200 ms, or two pulses 80 ms apart | out of rounds, knocked down, critical heat or peril |
| feel | 0.3-0.8, scaled by the event | 15-40 ms | shots, hits, blocks |
| buzz | 0.15-0.4, rising | repeated at the 60 ms rate limit | charging, holding an interaction, flamer stream |

Rules for every kind:
- **Rate limit.** A hand gets at most one pulse every 60 ms.
- **Notices win.** A notice is never dropped because a feel pulse just played.
- **Hands.** Pulses go to the hand concerned: the gun hand, or both hands
  while the gun is held two-handed; the hand reaching a zone; both hands for
  body events.
- **Scope.** Local player only, never while resimulating, never in menus
  except the menu kinds.

## How the done kinds are detected

- **Grip zone and grip taken:** the two-hand support module, as the support
  hand enters the acquire radius (not while held) and as the grip claim
  starts.
- **Holster armed:** the holster sampler, when a hand's request first becomes
  an acquirable offer for an equipped slot.
- **Shot:** the stock `_shoot` dispatch already observed by
  `darktidevr_ranged_evidence.lua`. DMF allows one hook per method, so the
  existing observer calls the haptics module. Covered: hit-scan, pellets,
  projectile, flamer gas and flamer burst actions.
- **Clip empty, reload, low ammo:** clip and reserve readings of the wielded
  ranged slot, compared each gameplay frame.
  - A reload is the clip going up while the reserve goes down, so an ammo
    pickup is not a reload.
  - A shell-by-shell reload pulses once, when the clip is full or the reserve
    is used up.
  - Low ammo is clip plus reserve falling to 20 % of capacity.

- **Body and abilities** (`31e1843`): one reading per gameplay frame of the
  local player, compared with the last. Pulses go to both hands, one per
  frame, taking the most important event the mode plays.
  - Health (`current_health`), for damage scaled by health lost against a
    quarter of maximum.
  - Toughness (`current_toughness_percent`), for broken, or lost without
    health loss.
  - Character state and `disabled_character_state`, for disabled.
  - The block component's `has_blocked` becoming true, for a block
    (perfect when `is_perfect_blocking`). One pulse per block hold, since
    stock clears it only when the hold ends.
  - `stamina.current_fraction` reaching 0.
  - Ability extension `remaining_ability_charges` for the combat ability
    (used or regained) and the grenade ability (regained).
  - A reading as the player becomes disabled reports only the disable, since
    the knocked-down health pool is not comparable.

## Transport

- **Game side:** Lua calls `dtvr_request_haptic_v1(hands, amplitude,
  duration_ms, frequency_hz)` in the capture library. The library counts
  requests on presentation state v7 and republishes at once.
- **Viewer:** plays one pulse per new count within a transport generation,
  with `xrApplyHapticFeedback` on each hand's haptic output (both hands are
  bound for Touch and simple controllers). It logs the first ten pulses and
  every failure kind.
- **Coalescing:** requests between two viewer frames coalesce to the latest.
  Pulses on one hand are 60 ms apart at most, so only simultaneous pulses on
  different hands can merge. Planned: a small queue if that shows up in play.
- **Current limit:** the viewer reads pulses where it reads presentation
  packets, which needs shared eyes. Menus and loading screens therefore get
  no pulses yet, and the menu kinds need that moved first.

## Order of work

Remaining kinds, in suggested order:
1. Melee hit (needs a hit observer; no component carries it).
2. Shot strength by weapon family.
3. Heat, charge and peril.
4. Interaction.
5. Menu kinds, after moving the pulse poll.

Also a strength slider (scale all amplitudes).

Each kind needs a unit test of its detection and an unattended log line; how
it feels is a worn check.
